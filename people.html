Xianhui (Kaye) Wang, Ph.D.

Principal Investigator · Assistant Professor

Dr. Wang joined the School of Speech, Language, and Hearing Sciences at San Diego State University in 2025. Her research examines how listeners understand speech in complex acoustic environments, how that ability varies across languages and clinical populations, and how auditory and cognitive processing interact during effortful listening.

Before joining SDSU, she was a postdoctoral scholar in the Department of Otolaryngology – Head and Neck Surgery at the University of California, Irvine. She holds a Ph.D. in Hearing Science from Ohio University, an M.Sc. in Audiological Science from University College London, and a B.Eng. in Biomedical Engineering from Capital Medical University.

Join Us

There are two ways to get involved with the lab: take part in a study, or join the team as a student research assistant.

Research participants

Our studies are reviewed and approved by the San Diego State University Institutional Review Board, and participants are paid for their time. Email Dr. Wang at kwang5@sdsu.edu to hear about ongoing opportunities.

Student research assistants

Fill in the form below whatever stage you are at — undergraduate, master’s, or doctoral. No prior research experience is needed; we will train you. You can also open the form in a new tab.

Join Us

The STC Lab is actively recruiting curious, motivated students. We work at the intersection of hearing science, speech perception, and cognition, and we welcome people from a range of backgrounds — speech and hearing sciences, psychology, linguistics, engineering, and computer science.

Graduate students

Prospective graduate students should apply through the School of Speech, Language, and Hearing Sciences at San Diego State University. If you are considering applying and think your interests align with the lab, please email Dr. Wang before you submit your application, with a short note about your research interests and a copy of your CV.

Undergraduate researchers

SDSU undergraduates can join the lab as research assistants. Typical responsibilities include helping run listening experiments, recruiting and scheduling participants, and processing speech and behavioral data. No prior research experience is required — we will train you. We ask for a commitment of at least two consecutive semesters where possible, so that you can move beyond training and contribute to a project meaningfully.

Research participants

Our studies involve listening to sounds and speech and responding to what you hear, usually in a single session on the SDSU campus. We recruit adults with typical hearing as well as listeners with hearing loss, tinnitus, or cochlear implants. If you are interested in taking part, please get in touch.

Get in touch

Email kwang5@sdsu.edu. For prospective students, it helps to include a brief description of your background, what draws you to this research, and your CV.
